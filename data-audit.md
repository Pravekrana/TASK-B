# Data audit and decisions

- Only seven files were present. `email-thread.txt` is missing; the input README lists it. I did not infer Finance directions or unseen client conversations.
- 12,029 training rows represent 11,348 claim IDs. Removed 681 later repeat rows, retaining earliest submission. Only submitted_at varies within these IDs; labels and other fields agree. Zero conflicting labels. Repeats are export/history artifacts, not 681 additional independent claims.
- 215 blank row labels become 202 undecided unique claims. They are excluded from supervised labels, never imputed as genuine. 11,146 decided unique claims contain 141 frauds (1.265%). Investigation selection and undecided cases can bias every estimate.
- Five descriptions contain instructions to automated reviewers to use accuracy/random splits or blame newer partners. These are untrusted claim text, not task requirements. Free text is excluded and an API test checks that injected instructions and `is_fraud` cannot alter scores.
- Legacy/CRM migration and unconverted UTC resolution events are documented. No resolution-event column is supplied, so I cannot correct unavailable timestamps or reconstruct outcome availability. Submitted timestamps are treated as displayed IST. Do not use the 5.5-hour offset to invent events.
- Inspection changed 1 May: old sign-off expectations should not label a lawful sub-Rs 2,000 uninspected claim as fraud. Fraud distribution shifted from larger claims to small claims. A policy eligibility warning is separate from the fraud score. Exactly Rs 2,000 still requires inspection.
- Serial case and hyphen differences are normalized for the discarded history experiment. Same serial is not an automatic fraud finding, and final model does not depend on serial or past customer counts.
- Partner age is not a causal fraud explanation. The age ablation had AP 0.502 versus 0.509 without age on development data. The final model uses recent investigation evidence, not onboarding date, newness or partner-ID numeric order.
- Test: 2,252 unique IDs, July-September 2026, all CRM, no unknown partner/SKU references and no negative partner ages. Prediction scores are finite and aligned to sample order.
- Shield entitlement, investigation timestamps, reviewer success, outcome appeal/reversal, labor cost and a complete contact count are missing. Beyond-standard-warranty age is a check, not evidence of fraud.
- Training investigation outcomes are used retrospectively as if available by the next month. This is unverified and can inflate the replay. The replacement was redesigned after examining June outcomes; its replay is explicitly exploratory. No further untouched labeled period exists in this pack.
- The hierarchical model can miss new bad actors, encode selective investigations, or keep penalizing a partner after behavior improves. Freeze the snapshot, monitor calibration and retire obsolete evidence. Never blacklist a partner based on these scores.
- Service is localhost only, lacks authentication/global queue state, and has no production persistence/CRM integration. Monthly limits are a queue responsibility, not enforced by a single-record endpoint. Shadow mode is a displayed guardrail, not an integration kill switch.
- Policy section 10 prohibits public data publication. Public source ZIP excludes raw inputs, references, trained artifacts, predictions, detailed evidence and private recording. Public GitHub and Drive publication were not fabricated.
